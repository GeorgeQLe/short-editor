SiftCut distributes FFmpeg 8.1.2 configured with --enable-gpl and
libx264. The resulting FFmpeg binaries are distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later.
The build also statically includes MIT-licensed FreeType and HarfBuzz for
caption rendering and text shaping.

The complete GPL text is available at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.txt.
The exact configure flags and corresponding-source instructions are recorded in
docs/release-sources.md and scripts/release/build-ffmpeg-macos-arm64.sh.
