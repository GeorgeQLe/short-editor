# Lessons

## 2026-07-31 — Make the product workflow visible in the brand mark

- SiftCut's defining output is a vertical top/bottom video composition. The
  film-cut mark should reflect that orientation instead of defaulting to a
  conventional horizontal filmstrip.
- Preserve this decision in executable branding coverage: the vector master
  must describe a vertical film frame and retain its clockwise 90-degree
  transform.
- When a palette has no established brand meaning, present intentional color
  alternatives and their tradeoffs before treating the current colors as a
  permanent semantic system.
